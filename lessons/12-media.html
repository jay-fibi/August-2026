Audio & Video

Native media is built into HTML: audio and video elements play files without any plugins, controlled by the controls attribute.

Lesson 12 Intermediate

💡 The Concept

  • controls — shows play/pause, volume and scrub bar. Without it the media is silent and invisible.
  • autoplay — starts automatically (often blocked by browsers; use muted too for reliability).
  • loop — restarts when finished; poster — a cover image shown before video plays.
  • source — list several formats; the browser plays the first it supports. Text inside the tag is a last-resort fallback.
💡 Best practice: Always include captions or a transcript, and never use autoplay for audio — it's jarring and often blocked.

📝 Source Code

audio and video with multiple sources and fallbacks 
<audio controls>
  <source src="song.mp3" type="audio/mpeg">
  <source src="song.ogg" type="audio/ogg">
  Your browser does not support audio.
</audio>

<video controls width="400" poster="cover.jpg">
  <source src="movie.mp4" type="video/mp4">
  <source src="movie.webm" type="video/webm">
  Your browser does not support video.
</video>

✅ Key Takeaways

Remember

  • controls is required for users to control media.
  • Provide multiple source formats for browser compatibility.
  • Always include fallback text and a poster for videos.
  • Avoid autoplaying audio; mute autoplaying video or don't autoplay at all.
